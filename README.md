# GLYC-Policy

GLYC-Policy is a research framework that integrates bioinformatics, artificial intelligence, behavioral nudging, and dynamic policy simulation to improve Type-2 Diabetes prevention in Indonesia.

## Overview

Current diabetes prevention programs mainly rely on generalized interventions. GLYC-Policy proposes a precision public health approach by combining:

- Genetic Risk Assessment
- AI-based Behavioral Nudging
- Dynamic Policy Simulation
- Ethical AI Governance

The framework is designed to support evidence-based policy decisions and personalized preventive interventions.

## Objectives

- Improve early identification of diabetes risk.
- Personalize preventive health interventions.
- Support government policy optimization.
- Reduce long-term healthcare expenditure.
